Overview

At Bike Rental, safety isn’t just a feature—it’s our foundation. We follow strict maintenance schedules, perform regular vehicle inspections, and ensure every trip meets high safety standards.

For Customers

We ensure every customer enjoys a safe and stress-free ride. Our bikes are sanitized after each trip, helmets are provided, and live GPS tracking ensures real-time monitoring during rides.

For Captains

Our captains undergo training sessions focusing on road safety, customer service, and emergency handling. Regular vehicle checks and health monitoring ensure both rider and captain safety.
